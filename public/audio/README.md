Drop your licensed calm audio here for full Safari support.

Suggested filename: `calm.mp3` (MP3 is required for Safari; OGG only plays in
Chrome/Firefox/Edge).

Then set in your `.env` (and rebuild — NEXT_PUBLIC_ vars are inlined at build):

    NEXT_PUBLIC_AMBIENT_AUDIO_URL=/audio/calm.mp3

Keep it low-volume, gentle, and loop-friendly. Any MP3/OGG/WebM is fine; only
use audio you have the rights to.
